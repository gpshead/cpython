Fix :mod:`asyncio` socket and TLS transports sending the current contents of
a :class:`bytearray` or writable :class:`memoryview` instead of the data
passed to :meth:`~asyncio.WriteTransport.write` or
:meth:`~asyncio.WriteTransport.writelines` when the object was modified
before the data had been sent, and looping forever if it was emptied.
